04 · The architect

The architect

He shaped Melbourne's skyline.Now it is Geelong's turn.

Callum Fraser has spent more than two decades shaping the way Melbourne meets the sky. Corio Quarter is the next edition of Geelong.

His idea here is a garden city lifted on light. A glass pavilion holds the street, the sport hall and the ground plane, open on every side. Above it, two towers carry planted terraces floor by floor, so the green climbs the building rather than sitting beside it. The roof of the pavilion becomes a garden for the people who live above it.

09 · ACTS Arena

ACTS Arena

A civic anchor,lit seven days a week.

A dedicated basketball facility in the podium: two full courts, 24 shooting bays with ball return technology, and raised seating for about 300. A glass roof opens over the court. Tournaments, leagues, school programs and youth clinics, run by a specialist operator.

The Events Centre

The same hall,set for an evening.

A stage, tables and the glass roof overhead: awards nights, performances and dinners in the middle of the city. One room, two lives. Event hire is not in the base case, so the Events Centre is upside.
