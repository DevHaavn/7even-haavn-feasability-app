02 · The Vision
A rental community built
like a private estate.
St Village Preston is a $321 million build-to-rent landmark set on the park — its podium and every home above commanding uninterrupted views across parkland to the Melbourne CBD skyline, just seven kilometres away. Four hundred premium build-to-rent apartments rise over a world-class ground plane: 1,500 m² of retail and food precinct, 1,500 m² of co-working, and more than 1,000 m² of wellness, day-spa and five-star hotel-grade facilities.
In a tightly planning-controlled pocket of Preston, it is a rare, amenity-led rental community delivered at institutional scale — single ownership, one operator, one standard. The Saints Row Members Club anchors the community across 2,911 m², complete with an indoor basketball court; the arched brick laneways below carry cafés, dining and daily life.
