// 05 How it goes together

Craned onto screw piles. Locked at the top corners.

Each unit arrives from the factory with the facade and terraces already fitted and is craned straight down onto screw piles, not slid sideways. Each pile carries a baseplate with an upward-facing locating pin that the unit is lifted onto, and permanent locking is at the top corners. On site after the units are set: flashings and cappings to the roof connections, then landscaping. Plant and equipment is installed on site too (not animated). Concept animation: piles, baseplates, pins, locks, cappings, crane and slings are schematic, not fabrication or installation instructions. The supplier and engineer must define supports, load paths, lifting, tolerances, locking hardware, joints and weather, fire and acoustic requirements.
