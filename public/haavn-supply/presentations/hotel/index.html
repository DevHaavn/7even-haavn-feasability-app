// 04 The modular room system

One room module, 35 m².

How the module works

  • Nominal room footprint 3.5 × 10 m, 35 m²
  • Rooms on opposite sides share one corridor
  • A 3.5 × 12 m strip includes the full corridor width
  • With rooms on both sides, the shared hall is allocated once
  • Walls and structure reduce the usable area

// 12 How it goes up

Podium, core, crane, 28 levels.

Illustrative construction sequence. The 30-storey illustration assumes two podium levels and 28 modular guest levels, built around one central concrete core. A separate 2 m corridor cassette sits beside the 3.5 × 10 m room pods, which avoids duplicating the shared hallway in the double-loaded plan; a room-plus-corridor strip measures 3.5 × 12 m nominal. Four locating pins per box, one at each corner and at every level, follow the design brief. Connector sizes, locking mechanisms, structural load paths, crane selection, fire design and site logistics remain to be engineered. This is not a construction method statement or an engineered connection design.
