// 09 How it goes up

Ground floor, core, crane, 24 levels.

Illustrative construction sequence. Ground floor plus 24 accommodation levels (25 storeys): a 4 m concrete ground floor, one central 12 × 12 m core, and on each level 28 studio modules of 3 × 8 m with 2 m corridors around the core. Four locating pins per module, one at each corner and at every level. How the corridor is formed (part of the module or a separate strip), connector sizes, structural load paths, crane selection, fire strategy and site logistics are not yet resolved. This is not a construction method statement or an engineered connection design.
