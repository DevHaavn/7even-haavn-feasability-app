03 · Why Geelong

Australia is shortof homes to rent.

When vacancy sits below two per cent, a market has no give in it. Rents rise, choice disappears and families move further out.

Governments have set bold targets. Delivery is behind them. The gap is the opportunity, and the people who fill it with well-run, long-term rental housing are the ones who will be paid for it.

06 · The numbers · Policy

The State backsbuild to rent.

Without the concession

~$1.6m

Land tax a year at the full rate, reducing yield and long-term hold returns.

With the build to rent concession

~$0.8m

A 50% reduction, about $800k saved a year, equivalent to around 18 basis points on the cap rate.

Eligibility: at least 50 dwellings, single ownership and management, held as build to rent for 15 years, completed by 31 December 2031.
