FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else, ask us in your message.

01 / 08

Do I need a planning permit?

Every site is different, and as part of our process we help you work out which permits yours will need. SOLUM Plus and SOLUM Studio are designed to comply with the secondary dwelling provisions, so in most residential zones they will not need a planning permit.

Some sites will still need one. It is best to confirm your situation with your local council or a town planner.

02 / 08

How do you approach compliance and quality control?

SOLUM Plus and SOLUM Studio are designed to exceed the National Construction Code minimum of 7 stars in all available regions. Both are designed to the NCC’s Liveable Housing Design requirements, with step-free entry, wider doorways and a bathroom designed for grab rails to be added later.

The final rating for your home will depend on your climate zone and how it sits on your site. Compliance is tracked through manufacture in our facility, so final sign-off is simple and efficient.

03 / 08

What does the process look like?

From the first conversation to the keys, in eleven or twelve stages depending on how your home reaches you. The way home sets out every one.

04 / 08

How does factory construction affect sustainability?

Building in a factory lets us achieve some of the most sustainable build outcomes in Australia. On average we reduce building waste by 78%, and a typical HAAVN build has a 45% reduction in embodied carbon and a 35% reduction in operational energy.

05 / 08

How do you handle warranties and defects?

Every HAAVN home comes with a manufacturer’s warranty, and there are checkpoints throughout to make sure yours arrives and is installed in good condition.

If a defect does arise, it is managed under the same warranty obligations and statutory standards that apply to any locally built home. It is no different because the home was manufactured offshore.

06 / 08

What is the lead time?

About 8 weeks from confirming your order for your home to arrive in Australia, ready for delivery. Allow around 3 weeks more for delivery and installation, depending on your location and local contractors.

07 / 08

How much does SOLUM weigh?

SOLUM Studio weighs approximately 17 tonnes, and SOLUM Plus approximately 25 tonnes.

Both figures are approximate. The final weight will vary with your fit-out and the furniture and appliances you choose, and we plan your delivery and craning around it.

08 / 08

Does HAAVN manage the whole process?

Yes. We offer an end-to-end service for every home we make. If you would rather, you can choose supply only, or supply and delivery, instead.
