01 · The project

The town centre of Australia's fastest-growing corridor.

575 Derrimut Road is a $500 million, 1,000-apartment master plan on 6.4 hectares at the corner of Leakes Road, Tarneit, the largest development in the area, in one of Australia's fastest-growing growth corridors. Sections 1 to 3 deliver six buildings and more than 120,000 m²: a Novotel hotel with 188 keys, function centre, restaurant, gym and pool; a dedicated health building pairing a doctors surgery and medical care with 150 aged-care suites; and four mixed-use buildings layering commercial offices, retail, food & beverage and apartments over an activated main-street ground plane. Tarneit railway station is minutes away, 25 minutes to Melbourne CBD, with Tarneit Central, schools and the recreation precinct at the doorstep.
