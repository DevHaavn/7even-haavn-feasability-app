// 03 The design

One planning framework. Two material schemes.

Urban Earth

Off-form concrete, black timber, pale stone and warm bronze. Richer texture and framed openings.

Simple Build

Olive sheet cladding, black metal and burgundy accents. Standard components and simpler detailing.

Both concepts retain the single-storey brief, transparent glass-roof links and level access. Simple Build is an economical design direction, not a priced saving.

// 08 How it goes together

Craned onto screw piles. Locked at the top corners.

Confirmed by client: the 2 m corridor is factory-attached to one module; modules are craned straight down onto screw piles, not slid sideways; the locating pins are vertical and downward; permanent locking is at the top corners. Concept animation: screw-pile number, spacing and embedment, pins, locks, crane and slings are schematic, not fabrication or installation instructions. The supplier and engineer must define supports, load paths, temporary stability, lifting, tolerances, locking hardware, joints and weather, fire and acoustic requirements.

// 09 The connections

Downward pins, screw piles and top-corner locks

Craned straight downVertical lowering only. Module B stays over its final position and lowers onto its pile-head receivers.
Downward locating pinsPins under both module sides lower vertically into pile-head receivers; no sideways insertion.
Top-corner locksSecure the upper corner interfaces after modules are seated. Lock hardware is schematic.

Pin gender and receiver hardware should be checked against supplier detail. Screw-pile number, spacing, embedment and cap sizes, pins, locks, crane slings and all connections are schematic only. Exact module support and connector design must be provided by the modular supplier and structural engineer.
