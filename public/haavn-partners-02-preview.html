Operating Directive · Confidential

Homes to market. The standard.

The operating framework for bringing SOLUM and MAGNA to the Australian market under the HAAVN × Hamilton Marino delivery model — one team, one sequence, one definition of ready. HAAVN enters as a precision, high-quality luxury manufacturer of homes — and SOLUM is the first thing anyone will ever judge us on.

A

The eight priorities

Where two things compete for the same hour, the higher number yields.
01

Quality is the gate. The date is the target.

We do not ship a compromised first product to protect a calendar. Slipping a date is survivable; a defective flagship is not.

02

SOLUM is the flagship and the proof.

The first HAAVN product ever released. Everything the market believes about MAGNA and every product after is inferred from SOLUM. Resourced, decided and protected first.

03

One accountable technical line: James Winstanley.

Design, engineering, technical direction and procurement direction sit with the Design Director — not distributed, not consensus-run.

04

HAAVN × Hamilton Marino is one entity to the customer.

The customer buys from HAAVN Black. Internally the manufacturing, install, warranty and insurance capability is the joint delivery model. The customer never manages the seam — we do.

05

Three ways to buy. One standard of build.

Supply Only, Supply & Install, and Hybrid Managed are commercial pathways — not quality tiers. The dwelling that leaves the factory is identical regardless of phase purchased.

06

Pricing is a system, not a document.

All pricing, freight and phase configuration is built and maintained inside ATRIUM. Figures issued outside ATRIUM are not HAAVN pricing.

07

Warranty and aftersales are designed before the first sale.

Support model, response times and the escalation path must be operational on the day the website takes its first enquiry — not built reactively after the first defect.

08

The brand is the constraint on everything.

Every touchpoint reads as a luxury precision manufacturer. Anything that does not meet that reading is rejected regardless of cost or schedule saving.
