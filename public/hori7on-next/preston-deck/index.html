02 · Why

Australia is shortof homes to rent.

When vacancy sits below two per cent, a market has no give in it. Rents rise, choice disappears and families move further out.

Governments have set bold targets. Delivery is behind them. The gap is the opportunity, and the people who fill it with well-run, long-term rental housing are the ones who will be paid for it.

A Saint Village rent is not just a home. It includes membership of a hotel-grade gym and bath house, a residents' work lounge and co-working floor, building-wide wifi, a concierge and parcel service, and maintenance handled on site by the operator.
