05 · The deal · Risks

What could go wrong.And what we have done about it.

RiskMitigant
Planning approvalDFP Category 1. Issues worked through with DTP before lodgement. The Minister decides; no third-party VCAT appeals.
Construction costBuilder $160m; independent QS DrawDown Partners $168.1m, about 5% higher, confirms the builder's sum is reasonable. The builder's price is fixed under Hamilton Marino's offer of 2 September 2026; the fixed-price period and exclusions are per the building contract, so the 5% contingency ($8.5m) remains available.
Cost escalationBuilder's price is fixed under Hamilton Marino's offer of 2 September 2026; the fixed-price period and exclusions are per the building contract, so no escalation allowance is required. DrawDown's 5% a year applies only if the start date moves beyond the fixed-price period.
Contamination2,500 tonnes already removed. Audit statement expected Q2 2027. Environmental line and contingency in TDC.
Interest ratesModelled at current rates, 8.0% all-in. Each 0.5% is ~$1.9m. Equity goes in first, so debt only starts around month 7.
Debt sizingConventional LVR 74% on BDO's value, above the usual 60 to 65%; a 65% cap needs ~$22m more equity. About $10m of senior debt relies on post-permit land equity, subject to lender acceptance and an independent land valuation.
Sale timingSold before or on completion; no post-completion hold. Leasing starts during construction, so the asset is partly leased at sale. Interest cover 1.0× to 1.2× at stabilisation if a refinance is needed.
LeasingApartment pre-leasing 6 months before completion; BDO allows 13 months to stabilise. Shops pre-leased ~2028; BDO's $8.95m vacant value is the floor.
Club demandThe $325m assumes 800 outside gym members (best case), which needs operator confirmation. The 400-member base case gives $319.5m and $58.4m profit.
Sale value$325m is income ÷ BDO's cap rates, stabilised and fully leased, with apartments incl. GST. At BDO's 98% occupancy, $315.0m. At 5.0%, investors still receive 1.6×. On the operator's view, 1.7×.
Foreign buyer stamp dutyA foreign buyer pays Victoria's 8% foreign purchaser additional duty, about $24m on the apartments, which would reduce the price they can pay. Forward funding or a sale agreed before completion avoids it.
GST treatmentApartment sale modelled with GST remitted ($27.4m); retail and Saints Row as a going concern. To be confirmed by tax adviser before financial close.
Land settlementSettlement (~March 2027, $13m balance) is not conditional on the permit. The permit is expected October to November 2026, about four months ahead.
