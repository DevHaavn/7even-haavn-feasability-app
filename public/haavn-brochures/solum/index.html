01 · The name

so·lum

/ˈsəʊ.ləm/  ·  Latin  ·  noun
  1. IThe ground. The floor. The base on which a thing stands.
  2. IIThe soil itself, and its living layer: the upper ground where roots take hold.

We gave the smallest home in the range the oldest word for ground. SOLUM is where HAAVN BLACK begins: a single module, set lightly on its site and made to belong to the place it stands on. Nothing added for the sake of it. Everything resolved.

HAAVN BLACK · SOLUM03
02 · HAAVN BLACK

Luxury is certainty.

A house is still built the way it was a hundred years ago. On a muddy site, by hand, one at a time. HAAVN BLACK begins from the opposite idea. The home is designed as a product, resolved completely before anything is made, then manufactured in a factory where a millimetre matters and weather does not. What arrives on site is not a frame. It is the home.

01

Resolved, not adjusted

Every junction, service and finish is settled in design. Nothing is decided later with a saw in hand, so nothing drifts.

02

Made under a roof

Modules are built in a controlled factory to tolerances a building site cannot hold, then transported complete.

03

Certain on site

Install is measured in days, not seasons. Less risk, less finance, less of the mess that makes building painful.

I dreamed of a space that speaks simply, that breathes with the land around it and still belongs to the life of the city. HAAVN BLACK is that idea, carried from the first line to the last detail, so the home you walk into is exactly the home that was imagined.

09 · Specification

What arrives.

SOLUM is manufactured whole as one module and delivered complete. What follows is the home as designed.

FormA single factory finished module, 12.6 × 4.2 m on a 4.2 m structural grid, raised on posts. 13.4 × 4.5 m overall.
FacadeFour finishes: Yakisugi charred timber, Blackbutt timber, Charcoal standing seam or Equitone fibre cement. Charcoal windows, flashings and trims throughout.
GlazingFull height glazing to the living, dining and bedroom. Bronze tinted, or silver with the Equitone facade.
KitchenGalley kitchen in dark timber with integrated finger pulls. Induction cooktop and dishwasher drawer, sink, drawers, pantry and fridge. Stainless steel and overhead shelving over a mosaic tile splashback.
LivingLiving room at the end of the module, opening to the deck option. Dark timber joinery with a fireplace and its own joinery, full height cupboards, satin chrome accents and bespoke spotlights.
BedroomBedroom with full height wardrobes, open shelves and a bed niche.
BathroomWalk in shower, a recessed half height pedestal basin integrated into the mosaic shelf, wall hung toilet with a niche above.
ServicesWasher and dryer, and a dedicated install cupboard.
Interior paletteTwo palettes over the same dark timber ceiling, stone walls and floor: 01 burgundy mosaic, or 02 green mosaic.
OptionsDeck, entry deck, outdoor kitchen with barbecue joinery, fridge and sink, rear entry.
