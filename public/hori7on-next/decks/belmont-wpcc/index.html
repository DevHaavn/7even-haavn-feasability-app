Belmont Estate · 48 Waurnvale Drive, Belmont · Prepared for Waurn Ponds Community Church

What the project must achieve to be viable.

The message

  1. The church’s goals are a new church and childcare centre, plus passive income.
  2. To pay for the church and still be fundable, the project must make a 17% margin on cost.
  3. With a modular build, the costs are as low as they can realistically go.
  4. So the homes must sell for these prices: 2-bed $953k, 3-bed $1.10m, 4-bed $1.19m (average $1.05m).
  5. Today’s sales evidence does not support these prices. The best townhouse sale nearby is $800k.
  6. The project is viable only if we can create a product and a market that achieve these prices.

What has to be true

  1. Sale prices: average $1.05m (the critical target).
  2. Modular build at a fixed price of $2,600/m², with signed inclusions.
  3. Civil infrastructure held to $9.7m.
  4. Church and childcare designed and built to a fixed budget of $13.0m.
  5. Finance kept low through a short modular programme.

If the prices can’t be reached

  • A smaller church (about 1,520 m²) lowers the target average to $1.01m.
  • The church funds part of its building.
  • A different scheme: sell serviced lots, keep the childcare for income, build the church in stages.

Next steps

  1. Church confirms its priorities: church size, childcare, income.
  2. Market test of the target prices (product, design, buyer demand).
  3. Fixed-price modular quote and civil cost plan.
  4. Decision on whether to proceed.
