01 · The Opportunity
Australia's first 5IVE.
Marriott Bonvoy on the beachfront.
5IVE Caloundra is the first five-star branded hotel on the Sunshine Coast — a Callum Fraser–designed, eight-storey beachfront landmark on the edge of Bulcock Beach, delivering 120 luxury residence hotel rooms with a gross value on completion of $125 million.

5IVE is backed and managed by Marriott Bonvoy — the world's largest hotelier, opening a new hotel every 48 hours with more than 10,000 hotels worldwide — joining its Autograph Collection alongside St Regis, EDITION, The Ritz-Carlton and W Hotels.

From a 1,042 m² double-frontage site, the hotel steps out over the water with a 1,000 m² elevated bar above the ocean and a combined drop-off and arrival, crowned by a rooftop pool, fire pit and bar. Caloundra is one of three host locations for the villages of the 2032 Australian Olympic Games.
