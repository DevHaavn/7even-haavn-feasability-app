Editor's note
A rental community built like a private estate.
St Village Preston is a $321 million build-to-rent landmark set on the park — its podium and every home above commanding uninterrupted views across parkland to the Melbourne CBD skyline, seven kilometres away. Four hundred premium residences rise over a world-class ground plane: 1,500 m² of retail and food precinct, 1,500 m² of co-working and more than 1,000 m² of wellness, day-spa and five-star hotel-grade facilities. In a tightly planning-controlled pocket of Preston it is a rare, amenity-led rental community at institutional scale — single ownership, one operator, one standard. The numbers in this memorandum are live outputs of the 7EVEN ATRIUM feasibility engine.
