01 — Directive

What comes first, and why.

This is not a wish list. It is the order of priority set by the Director of HAAVN BLACK. Where two things compete for the same hour, the higher-numbered priority yields. Every decision James's team makes between now and launch should be traceable back to one of these eight lines.

The single sentence: HAAVN enters the Australian market as a precision, high-quality luxury manufacturer of homes — and SOLUM is the first thing anyone will ever judge us on.

01

Quality is the gate. The date is the target.

We do not ship a compromised first product to protect a calendar. If a gate cannot be closed at the required standard, it escalates to the Director the day it is known — not the week before launch. Slipping a date is survivable. A defective flagship is not.

02

SOLUM is the flagship and the proof.

SOLUM is the first HAAVN product ever released. Everything the market believes about MAGNA, and about every product after it, will be inferred from SOLUM. It is resourced first, decided first and protected first.

03

One accountable technical line: James Winstanley.

Design, engineering, technical direction, design management and procurement direction sit with the Design Director. Not distributed, not consensus-run. James's team may delegate execution; it does not delegate the decision.

04

HAAVN × HAMILTON MARINO is one entity to the customer.

The customer buys from HAAVN BLACK. Internally the manufacturing, install, warranty and insurance capability is the HAAVN × HAMILTON MARINO joint delivery model. The customer never manages the seam between them — we do.

05

Three ways to buy. One standard of build.

Supply Only, Supply & Install, and Hybrid Managed are commercial pathways — not quality tiers. The dwelling that leaves the factory is identical in specification, finish and tolerance regardless of which phase the customer purchased.

06

Pricing is a system, not a document.

All pricing, freight, and phase configuration is built and maintained inside ATRIUM, the HAAVN online application. This directive defines what must be priced and how it is structured. It deliberately carries no figures. Figures issued outside ATRIUM are not HAAVN pricing.

07

Warranty and aftersales are designed before the first sale.

The support model, response times, and the escalation path into the HAAVN Management team must be operational on the day the website takes its first enquiry — not built reactively after the first defect.

08

The brand is the constraint on everything.

Materials, tolerances, packaging, delivery presentation, documentation, the website, the contract, the handover — every touchpoint reads as a luxury precision manufacturer. Anything that does not meet that reading is rejected regardless of cost or schedule saving.

04 — Purchase Model

Three ways to buy a HAAVN home.

The same dwelling, delivered under three different levels of HAAVN responsibility. Each phase must be independently priced for both products in every launch state. Scope boundaries below are the contract boundaries — they define exactly where HAAVN's obligation stops and the customer's begins.

Phase01

Supply Only

The manufactured dwelling delivered to the customer's site. HAAVN's obligation ends at set-down. No permits, no install, no external works. For customers with their own builder, or developers running their own program.

SOLUM & MAGNA

HAAVN Supplies

  • Complete manufactured dwelling to HAAVN specification
  • Factory quality certification and benchmark compliance
  • Engineering and shop documentation pack
  • Transport to nominated site address
  • Craneage / set-down onto customer-prepared base
  • Transit insurance to point of set-down
  • Manufacturer's product warranty on the dwelling

Explicitly Excluded

  • Building permit and any planning approvals
  • Site survey, soil testing, site preparation
  • Footings, slab or any base works
  • Service connections — power, water, sewer, gas, data
  • Installation, tie-down, final fix or commissioning
  • Occupancy Permit and any certification of the completed works
  • Driveways, paths, decks, landscaping, fencing
  • All other external and site works of any kind

Pricing Inputs Required

  • Ex-works dwelling value per product
  • Freight rate per delivery zone
  • Craneage and set-down allowance by access class
  • Transit insurance provisioning
  • Product warranty provisioning
  • Escort / permit costs for oversize loads
  • Price validity period and escalation trigger
Boundary risk Set-down onto a base HAAVN did not build creates a defect-attribution risk. James's team must issue a mandatory base tolerance specification and a customer sign-off before dispatch, or Phase 1 warranty exposure is uncontrolled.
Phase02

Supply & Install — Turnkey to Occupancy

HAAVN × HAMILTON MARINO supply, install, insure and warrant the dwelling to the point of Occupancy Permit. The bare minimum required for a legally occupiable, fully finished turn-key home — and nothing beyond it.

Primary Offer

HAAVN Supplies

  • Everything in Phase 01
  • Building permit and required certifications for the dwelling
  • Site establishment and set-out
  • Footings / base system to engineered design
  • Full installation, tie-down, seal and weatherproofing
  • Service connections from dwelling to nominated service points
  • Internal completion, final fix and commissioning
  • Statutory domestic building insurance for the state of works
  • Works and public liability insurance during installation
  • Achievement of Occupancy Permit
  • Full HAAVN warranty on dwelling and installation
  • Handover, documentation pack and homeowner induction

Explicitly Excluded

  • Driveways, crossovers, paths and paving
  • Landscaping, turf, gardens, retaining
  • Decking, pergolas, carports, sheds
  • Pools, water tanks, solar beyond base spec
  • Extension of services beyond the nominated connection point
  • Demolition, tree removal, rock excavation, contaminated soil
  • Planning / town planning applications where required
  • Any works to the existing primary dwelling on the lot
  • All other external works of any kind

Pricing Inputs Required

  • All Phase 01 inputs
  • Permit and certification cost by state
  • Site establishment by access and slope class
  • Base / footing system by soil classification
  • Installation crew cost by region
  • Service connection allowance and PC-sum method
  • Statutory building insurance premium by state
  • Works, liability and defects insurance
  • Warranty reserve per unit
  • Provisional sums, exclusions register and variation rates
Open decision — fencing The Director's brief reads "no driveway or any other works excluding fencing." This document has been written with fencing excluded from Phase 02. If the intent is that fencing is included, James must confirm in writing before ATRIUM configuration — the two readings carry materially different cost and contract scope.
Phase03

Hybrid Supply & HAAVN Managed Build Support

HAAVN supplies the dwelling; the customer's own builder or nominated trades perform the installation under HAAVN technical direction, staged inspection and management. Warranty is apportioned by scope, not assumed wholesale.

Managed

HAAVN Supplies

  • Everything in Phase 01
  • HAAVN installation methodology and technical specification
  • Builder induction, accreditation and briefing
  • Design management and technical direction through install
  • Nominated hold points and staged HAAVN inspections
  • Remote and on-site technical support to the installing builder
  • Compliance review of installed works against HAAVN standard
  • Sign-off certificate on satisfactory completion
  • Dwelling warranty retained by HAAVN; install warranty by the builder

Explicitly Excluded

  • Physical installation labour and plant
  • Builder's permits, licences and insurances
  • All site, civil and external works
  • Programme and cost risk on the builder's scope
  • Warranty on works performed outside HAAVN control
  • Rectification arising from non-compliance with HAAVN method

Pricing Inputs Required

  • All Phase 01 inputs
  • Management fee — fixed or percentage basis
  • Technical direction hours by product
  • Inspection visits — number, cost and travel by region
  • Builder accreditation and induction cost
  • Documentation and methodology licence value
  • Additional / re-inspection rate card
  • Apportioned warranty reserve
Structural risk Phase 03 is where brand damage is most likely: a third party's poor install becomes a HAAVN-branded home. Accreditation must be a gate, not a formality — HAAVN retains the right to refuse a builder and to withhold the sign-off certificate. James to define the minimum accreditation standard before launch.

09 — After the Sale

Warranty and support, built before launch.

A luxury manufacturer is judged on what happens after the money changes hands. The support model must be live and staffed on the day haavnblack.com.au takes its first enquiry.

Stage 01

Handover

Documentation pack, warranty register, homeowner induction, defect inspection at completion, digital record created in ATRIUM.

Stage 02

Defects Period

Defined defects liability window with published response and rectification times. Scheduled check-in inspections, not customer-initiated only.

Stage 03

Structural Warranty

Statutory structural and non-structural warranty per state, plus HAAVN's own manufacturer warranty on the dwelling. Apportioned in Phase 03.

Stage 04

Ongoing Care

Single support channel into the HAAVN Management team. Parts and finishes supply, service visits, and a feedback loop back into engineering.

What James's team must define

  • Warranty scope and duration by phase and by state
  • Published response and rectification times
  • Warranty apportionment rules for Phase 03 builder installs
  • Escalation path and authority to approve rectification spend
  • Spare parts, finishes and component continuity plan
  • Warranty reserve per unit, fed into ATRIUM pricing

Channel — haavnblack.com.au

The website is the sales channel, not a brochure. Before launch it must carry:

  • SOLUM product release with the three purchase phases clearly separated
  • State selector that only accepts enquiries from licensed markets
  • Enquiry-to-order flow connected to ATRIUM pricing
  • Delivery zone and site-access qualification before quoting
  • Owner support entry point for the aftersales desk

Statutory warranty periods, licensing requirements and home warranty insurance obligations differ between Victoria, Queensland and New South Wales and change over time. The positions above are structural, not legal advice — each must be confirmed with the relevant state regulator and HAAVN's legal and insurance advisers before any contract is issued.
