01 · The why

A house is still built the way it was
a hundred years ago. On a muddy site,
by hand, one at a time.

HAAVN BLACK begins from the opposite idea. The home is designed as a product, resolved completely before anything is made, then manufactured in a factory where a millimetre matters and weather does not. What arrives on site is not a frame. It is the home.

01

Resolved, not adjusted

Every junction, service and finish is settled in design. Nothing is decided later with a saw in hand, so nothing drifts.

02

Made under a roof

Modules are built in a controlled factory to tolerances a building site cannot hold, then transported complete.

03

Certain on site

Install is measured in days, not seasons. Less risk, less finance, less of the mess that makes building painful.

02 · Design & materials

Black is
a discipline.

A dark, quiet envelope. Charred timber, stone and steel, with warmth held inside. The palette is deliberately short: fewer materials, better ones, detailed properly.

03 · The range

Ten homes.
One system.

The Black Series is the architectural line, from a single module to a fourteen module residence. Essentials brings the same manufacturing to standard land lots.

04 · How it is made

Built inside.
Finished outside.

Modules leave the line complete: services in, linings on, kitchens and bathrooms fitted. On site a crane sets them in place and the home is weather tight the same week.
