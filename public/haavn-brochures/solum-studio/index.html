01 · The name

so·lum

/ˈsəʊ.ləm/  ·  Latin  ·  noun
  1. IThe ground. The floor. The base on which a thing stands.
  2. IIThe soil itself, and its living layer: the upper ground where roots take hold.

SOLUM STUDIO is the compact expression of the idea. A 3.5 by 10 metre studio, 35 square metres gross, made as a single module with a glass front, a bedroom, a bathroom and a full kitchen. Set lightly on its site. Nothing added for the sake of it. Everything resolved.

HAAVN BLACK · SOLUM STUDIO03
02 · HAAVN BLACK

Luxury is certainty.

A house is still built the way it was a hundred years ago. On a muddy site, by hand, one at a time. HAAVN BLACK begins from the opposite idea. The home is designed as a product, resolved completely before anything is made, then manufactured in a factory where a millimetre matters and weather does not. What arrives on site is not a frame. It is the home.

01

Resolved, not adjusted

Every junction, service and finish is settled in design. Nothing is decided later with a saw in hand, so nothing drifts.

02

Made under a roof

Modules are built in a controlled factory to tolerances a building site cannot hold, then transported complete.

03

Certain on site

Install is measured in days, not seasons. Less risk, less finance, less of the mess that makes building painful.

03 · The vision
A home should feel like a deep breath.

I dreamed of a space that speaks simply, that breathes with the land around it and still belongs to the life of the city. HAAVN BLACK is that idea, carried from the first line to the last detail, so the home you walk into is exactly the home that was imagined.

Jamie BaldwinFounder · HAAVN BLACK

Nothing is lost between the drawing and the home.

Simplicity is the hardest thing to build. We designed SOLUM STUDIO exactly as it is made, to the millimetre, so the home that arrives is the home we drew. It works from the first day, and it never stops feeling like architecture.

13 · Specification

What arrives.

SOLUM STUDIO is manufactured whole as one module and delivered complete. What follows is the studio as designed.

FormA single factory finished module, 10 × 3.5 m, 35 square metres gross.
FacadeFour finishes: Yakisugi black timber, Blackbutt timber, Black metal or Light concrete. Black windows and trims throughout.
GlazingA full height glazed sliding front, 3,100 × 3,000 mm, opening the living space to the balcony. Full height side windows to the bedroom and dining, and a solid matte black side entry door.
KitchenA full kitchen with a silver drum hood over the cooktop. Mosaic tile splashback in your choice of colour.
LivingLiving and dining at the glass front. Timber ceiling planks and joinery in a matching timber, and a black three blade fan.
BedroomA bedroom with timber joinery and a black fan.
BathroomShower with a framed screen. Mosaic tile to the shower walls and the vanity.
Interior paletteFour tile colours, Sage, Tide, Burgundy and Vine, with two timber schemes, Eveneer Raw Walnut or Charcoal Oak, coordinated across the ceiling planks and the fixed joinery.

Renders are image studies and are not dimensionally exact to the architectural model. The exterior views show the burgundy interior. Furniture, landscaping and styling are shown for illustration and are not included. Material colour changes with light, so finishes are specified from physical samples. Final specification is confirmed at contract. Design by Fraser & Partners for HAAVN.
